What to include when you write

Your macOS version, the app version (Rainbow Workshop menu, then About), and what you were doing on which screen when the problem happened. A screenshot helps us find the cause much faster. We usually reply within two or three days.

Which devices does it run on?

Rainbow Workshop is a Mac app, available on the Mac App Store. Your plans sync through your own iCloud account, so they also show up in our companion iPhone app.

How much does it cost?

It is free to download, and weekly and daily plans, routines, to-dos, reviews, the timer and syncing with iPhone are all free. Things that only appear once past weeks pile up (eight weeks of review trends, bringing in another week’s plan, exporting reviews and sharing your schedule) are part of Rainbow Workshop Pro, available as a yearly or monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Use Restore Purchase in Settings to get a purchase back on another Mac, and Redeem Code to enter a promo code.

I have an idea for a feature.

Please send it. You can also open Settings, then Feedback & review, to see the features coming next and suggest your own. Many updates start from these messages.

Where can I see what changed?

Each update shows what is new the first time you open the app, and you can see it again in Settings. Version notes are also on the App Store page.

Privacy

There is no sign-up and no login. We do not collect anything that identifies you, and what you write stays on your Mac and in your own iCloud account. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (such as the app version, how often it is opened and how many to-dos there are, never their content), and you can turn this off in Settings under Usage Statistics. Emails you send us are used only to reply to you.
